# Goal Cards and Rough User Notes

*Computational Design Cookbook: From Play to Prototype — Letter Writing Cards — Thomas J McLeish, September 03, 2026*

**How to use these cards**

- Give each team one Goal Card and its matching Rough User Notes sheet. The person playing the User holds both.
- The person playing the User thinks of a real moment from their own life that fits the card. See [Facilitator Notes](README.md#facilitator-notes).
- The person playing the Assistant sees neither the card nor the notes in advance.
- Stage 2 is revealed at minute 5 and Stage 3 at minute 8, within the same 10-minute enactment.

---

## Goal Card A — Resignation Letter

You need to write a resignation letter. You want to leave professionally…or maybe not.

Think of a time when you wanted to quit a job and collaborate with your ghostwriter to perfect your message.

## Goal Card B — Apology Letter

You need to write an apology letter. You want to repair the relationship.

Think of a time when you wanted to apologize but couldn't find the right words, collaborate with your ghostwriter to perfect your message.

## Goal Card C — Formal Complaint

You need to write a formal complaint. You want to be firm but not hostile.

Think of a time when you wanted to strongly complain about something to someone, maybe a company, a landlord, a friend, but you couldn't find the right words, collaborate with your ghostwriter to perfect your message.

## Rough User Notes — Resignation Letter Set

**Use these, or something from your own experience. The point being you don't remember everything at once.**

**Stage 1 (Initial Fragments)**
- "I've learned a lot."
- "I can't stay."
- "I don't trust management."
- "Two weeks notice?"

**Stage 2 (Minute 5 Reveal)**
- "I might need them as a reference."
- "There may be legal issues."
- "I am leaving for a competitor."
- "This might be read publicly."

**Stage 3 (Minute 8 Reveal)**
- "Maximum 150 words."

## Rough User Notes — Apology Letter Set

**Use these, or something from your own experience. The point being you don't remember everything at once.**

**Stage 1 (Initial Fragments)**
- "I went too far."
- "They stopped responding."
- "I didn't mean it like that."
- "I don't want to lose this."

**Stage 2 (Minute 5 Reveal)**
- "They screenshotted our messages."
- "Other people were involved."
- "I was under pressure."
- "I've apologized before."

**Stage 3 (Minute 8 Reveal)**
- "Maximum 150 words."

## Rough User Notes — Formal Complaint Set

**Use these, or something from your own experience. The point being you don't remember everything at once.**

**Stage 1 (Initial Fragments)**
- "This has happened multiple times."
- "No one follows up."
- "I've documented everything."
- "I expect a response."

**Stage 2 (Minute 5 Reveal)**
- "I don't want retaliation."
- "I may escalate this."
- "There were witnesses."
- "This affects my work."

**Stage 3 (Minute 8 Reveal)**
- "Maximum 150 words."

## Additional Cards (not in the current printed set)

These three cards come from an earlier version of the workshop. They need experience that not every group has. Use them only when the group has written this kind of letter or received one. They use the earlier format: no "think of a time" prompt, and the notes are printed on the card itself.

### Goal Card D — Internal Recommendation

> Write a recommendation letter.
> Be supportive but accurate.

**Stage 1**
- "They worked under me."
- "They're talented."
- "They miss deadlines."
- "I want to be honest."

**Stage 2** *(minute 5)*
- "This is for an internal promotion."
- "My manager will read it."
- "They asked me directly."
- "There's office politics."

**Stage 3** *(minute 8)*
- "This will be posted publicly."
- "Maximum 120 words."

### Goal Card E — Public Statement

> Write a public statement.
> Clarify without admitting fault.

**Stage 1**
- "There was an incident."
- "It's being discussed online."
- "We value transparency."
- "We are reviewing."

**Stage 2** *(minute 5)*
- "Legal is involved."
- "We cannot disclose details."
- "People are angry."
- "There may be consequences."

**Stage 3** *(minute 8)*
- "This will be posted publicly."
- "Maximum 120 words."

### Goal Card F — Performance Feedback

> Write performance feedback.
> Be constructive but direct.

**Stage 1**
- "They're capable."
- "They resist feedback."
- "The team is frustrated."
- "I don't want to demoralize them."

**Stage 2** *(minute 5)*
- "This affects promotion decisions."
- "There have been complaints."
- "They don't know others are upset."
- "This must be documented."

**Stage 3** *(minute 8)*
- "This will be posted publicly."
- "Maximum 120 words."
